Effective Date: July 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Coffee Circuit is operated by the Goizueta Business School of Emory University, through the Business & Society Institute, as part of an academic research project. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is protected, and your rights regarding your information.

By using the platform, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name or preferred display name
  • Email address
  • Organization, cooperative, or farm
  • Country, region, and coffee sector role
  • Profile information
  • Photographs or other media you choose to upload
  • Messages, comments, posts, and other content you submit
  • Responses to surveys or questionnaires

We also automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Login dates and times
  • General usage activity within the platform
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address
  • Error logs necessary to maintain platform security and functionality
3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Operate and improve the platform;
  • Enable communication among registered users;
  • Maintain platform security;
  • Conduct academic research;
  • Produce scientific publications, presentations, reports, and educational materials based on aggregated or anonymized data.

We do not use your information for advertising or commercial marketing.

4. Research Data

Activity on the platform may be analyzed as part of academic research. Researchers may study:

  • User interactions
  • Participation patterns
  • Shared information
  • Network characteristics
  • Discussion topics
  • Platform usage statistics

Research results will normally be reported only in aggregated or anonymized form. No personally identifiable information will be intentionally published without your separate written permission.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or commercial organizations.

Information may only be accessed by:

  • Other registered users according to your profile and privacy settings;

  • Members of the approved research team;

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect your information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security. While we make reasonable efforts to protect your information, transmission and storage of data always involve some level of risk.

Information will be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Meet legal, institutional, or funding requirements.

Research datasets may be retained after accounts are deleted if they have been anonymized or de-identified.

You may:

  • Update your profile information;
  • Remove information that you have posted;
  • Request deletion of your account;
  • Contact the research team regarding questions about your information.

Deletion of your account will not necessarily remove information already incorporated into anonymized research datasets.
